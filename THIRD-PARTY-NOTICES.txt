Distribution requirement
------------------------

Every final ClickGit release must retain and distribute the complete license
files, copyright notices, exceptions, and third-party notices supplied with
these components. This baseline does not prove that the required Qt and PySide6 license materials
are present in the application bundles or release archives. Before the next
release, the actual packaged modules and archives must be inspected, the
required materials must be collected, and their inclusion must be verified.
If that verification is incomplete, the release must be blocked.

docs/licenses must be created and maintained before the next release as the
project's license inventory location. The directory will be established by
the subsequent documentation-structure task; this notice does not claim that
it already exists. This summary is not a substitute for the complete license
materials and does not grant additional rights.
