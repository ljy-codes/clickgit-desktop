Distribution requirement
------------------------

Every final ClickGit release must retain and distribute the complete license
files, copyright notices, exceptions, and third-party notices supplied with
these components. Open-source distribution must retain the applicable LGPL
or GPL license texts, Qt/PySide6/Shiboken6 notice and source offer, Python
license, PyInstaller exception, and the notices retained inside PortableGit.

The project-maintained distributable texts are stored under
docs/licenses/distribution and are listed with SHA-256 digests in
LICENSE-MANIFEST.json. Windows and macOS build scripts run
scripts/verify_licenses.py before packaging and verify the copied package
again. If a declared dependency version, required file, packaged file set, or
SHA-256 digest differs, the release must be blocked.

docs/licenses is established as the project's license-governance entry point.
Its hash-locked distribution bundle must be reviewed whenever dependencies or
packaged modules change. Automated verification confirms the declared files
and versions; it is not a substitute for legal review and does not grant
additional rights.
